🚀 Overview

WorkSpace is a modern platform focused on building high-performance web and mobile applications for shared workspace environments. It addresses the friction of physical co-working hubs by optimizing calendar booking and desk-level IoT status controls.

💡 Core Philosophy

Every product is built with an intentional balance of functionality, design, and technology. The focus is not just on building an application, but on creating an engaging digital experience that solves physical space management problems.

⚙️ Key Features

Custom Workspace Management

Complete digital workflows for desk reservations and calendar bookings.

Scalable Architecture
